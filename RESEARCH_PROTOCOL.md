# Research Protocol

One bounded, preregistered task is selected per cycle. A final holdout is inaccessible until its data contract passes. Historical promotion requires the numerical gates in `ACCEPTANCE_GATES.yaml`; data blockers and failed experiments remain permanent ledger records. Every execution is zero-capital, research-only, and cannot place live or paper orders.

## Phase 2 authorization

Phase 1 is permanently preserved as `APPROVED_SPACE_EXHAUSTED / RESEARCH_BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`. Phase 2 is a separately authorized research phase, `ACTIVE_RESEARCH_PHASE_2`; it does not reopen, relabel, or soften any Phase 1 experiment or audit.

Phase 2 executes the exact queue in `PHASE_2_AUTHORIZATION.json`. Every experiment is bounded. Before a formal economic evaluation it requires a frozen preregistration, unit tests, production-path integration against exact hash-allowlisted development files, representative-row materialization, independent signal/target/fill/cost/return reconciliation, boundary and delayed-execution tests, a closed holdout latch, and implementation-fidelity review. Synthetic tests alone are insufficient.

Each Phase 2 experiment permits at most three pre-run implementation-repair rounds and at most two post-run fidelity-repair rounds for mechanical defects. Each independent review stage permits at most three audit attempts, but only a timeout, context exhaustion, or temporary service failure authorizes an audit retry. Such interruption is `AUDIT_PENDING`; a parser, gate-count, production-path, or trace defect is `IMPLEMENTATION_BLOCKED`, not an economic rejection. A family is consumed only by a contract-faithful economic no-go, a reproducible authorized-data no-go, or exhaustion of the explicit Phase 2 repair budget where the remaining defect cannot be fixed without materially changing the economic hypothesis.

The January--June 2026 holdout stays sealed until every development, fidelity, robustness, freeze, and hash gate passes. All previous observations count toward multiplicity. Phase 2 remains zero-capital: no trading, credentials, wallets, leverage, paid data, paid APIs, paid compute, credit purchase, automatic top-up, or autonomous financial action is permitted. Fixed-pair work uses CPU and Vertcoin mining remains unchanged.

## Phase 3 authorization

Phase 3 is separately authorized as `ACTIVE_RESEARCH_PHASE_3_ADAPTIVE_PORTFOLIO`. It preserves the exact Phase 1 final result and every Phase 2 artifact and terminal state. Its programme layers are discovery, validation, frozen prospective evaluation, and capital-readiness documentation. Discovery is bounded and exploratory; it cannot authorize a final claim, holdout access, prospective promotion, or capital. Validation retains the existing chronological, cost-aware, reproducible, hash-bound, and independent-audit standards. Prospective work uses data arriving after a freeze. Capital remains zero.

Independent workstreams do not globally block one another. One repository writer and the existing lock serialize mutations, but a route blocks only successors that depend on it. Service interruption does not consume substantive scientific review, and a mechanical defect is not an economic rejection. Results use only the labels registered in `PHASE_3_AUTHORIZATION.json`.

The first Phase 3 milestone is one clean development economic result, whether profitable or unprofitable. Until then, existing-data production execution has priority over broad data-route reviews, neural models, unbounded searches, publication polish, or infrastructure without immediate production use. Mean-reversion v2 has a new Phase 3 completion budget without altering its Phase 2 administrative terminal evidence or frozen economics. Before a Phase 3 mechanical round is consumed, the unchanged representative real-data path and a complexity benchmark must localize the blocker. If its bounded completion fails, relative-value v2 proceeds independently.

Route 4 v3 remains `DIRECTION_DESIGN_INCONCLUSIVE / NO_DATA_RESULT / NO_ECONOMIC_RESULT`, deferred rather than scientifically rejected. No sixth review occurs in the first Phase 3 cycle. A new programme-level design decision may occur only after a clean fixed-pair economic result.

The common portfolio contract starts with deterministic eligibility and risk rules. Cash is the default; every sleeve may receive zero. No unvalidated sleeve can activate, net edge must be assessed after costs and uncertainty, scenario selection requires separate validation, and a frozen sleeve cannot be retuned during prospective evaluation. The capital ladder is documentation only and activates no stage beyond zero capital.
