# RegimeMoE Program Charter

RegimeMoE is a persistent, lean controller for adaptive regime-aware mixtures of cryptocurrency trading experts under transaction costs. Phase 0 creates contracts and safe workflow only: capital is zero; no data acquisition, strategy evaluation, model training, GPU use, credentials, orders, or holdout access is permitted. Discovery is bounded and exploratory; validation and promotion gates are separate. Only a candidate that can qualify for promotion may receive a focused independent Sol audit. Historical holdout paths remain unresolved and unread pending separate human authorization.

The program balances DATA_AND_EVALUATION, EXPERTS_AND_BASELINES, AI_ROUTER, THESIS_AND_CAREER, and AUDIENCE_AND_MONETIZATION weekly. Content derives solely from verified milestones; it may not sell signals, returns, advice, or an unvalidated model.
