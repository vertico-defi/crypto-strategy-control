# Phase 3 portfolio operating system

This is a research contract, not a trading system. Capital eligibility is false for every sleeve until separately authorized prospective gates pass. Cash is the default and may remain the only allocation.

## Deterministic eligibility

A sleeve is eligible for research allocation only when its data contract, frozen implementation, historical validation, fidelity audit, and current health evidence all pass. A validated sleeve is still assigned zero when its scenario evidence is absent, its conservative expected edge does not exceed estimated fees, spread, slippage, and uncertainty, or any suspension rule fires. An unvalidated, retired, data-inconclusive, implementation-inconclusive, or degraded sleeve cannot be activated.

The scenario categories are directional trend, range or shock mean reversion, cross-asset dispersion, cross-sectional dispersion, positive carry, market stress, and insufficient evidence. Insufficient evidence always maps to cash. Any scenario selector is a distinct model or deterministic rule and must be preregistered and validated separately; it cannot be inferred from the final holdout.

## Risk and attribution

Before portfolio validation, a versioned allocation contract must freeze sleeve caps, aggregate exposure, volatility scaling, correlation treatment, turnover limits, drawdown limits, and abstention thresholds. No complex meta-model is authorized while fewer than two sleeves have independently validated evidence. A frozen sleeve cannot be retrained or retuned during prospective evaluation.

Every portfolio decision must retain the observation boundary, eligible and ineligible sleeves with reasons, scenario state, expected gross and net edge estimates, cost inputs, requested and capped weights, cash weight, exposure and risk checks, health state, executed research timestamp, and sleeve/portfolio attribution. Missing evidence fails closed to cash.

Health rules must be frozen per sleeve before prospective use and cover data freshness, realized-versus-expected costs, return degradation, drawdown, turnover, scenario mismatch, and structural implementation integrity. Threshold breaches reduce exposure to zero unless a preregistered intermediate reduction rule exists. Suspension cannot be cleared by ad hoc retuning.
